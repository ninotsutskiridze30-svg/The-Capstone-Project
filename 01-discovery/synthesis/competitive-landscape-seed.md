# Competitive Landscape — Initial Seed (Lab 4)

**Team:** TutorLink Team
**Date:** April 2, 2026

---

## Direct Competitors

### Preply
Global tutoring marketplace (1:1 video lessons). Not available in Georgian; no exam-specific syllabus coverage. Commission: 18–33%. No Georgia-specific subject filters.
**Gap vs our ICP:** English-first UI; no exam-syllabus fields; high commission discourages Georgian tutors.

### iTalki
Language learning marketplace. Commission: 15%. No Georgian exam-prep context; focused on language acquisition, not curriculum-based exam prep.
**Gap:** Designed for language acquisition, not academic exam prep.

### Tutorful (UK)
UK-focused; strong for GCSE/A-Level prep. Commission: 22%. No Georgian presence.
**Gap:** Entirely UK-centric.

### Superprof
Directory model — tutors list themselves, students contact directly. No booking or payment infrastructure.
**Gap:** Directory, not platform — same WhatsApp problem in a different wrapper.

---

## Indirect Competitors (Status Quo)

### WhatsApp / Telegram group chats
How all current tutor discovery actually happens in Georgia. Cost: free. Problem: no structure, stale information, manual negotiation, no reviews. **This is the behaviour we are replacing.**

### Facebook groups / marketplace
Secondary channel — tutors post self-advertisements. No booking, no payment, no reviews.

### Zoom
Used by tutors who have already found a student. No discovery, no booking, no homework integration. **We are not competing with Zoom on video — we replace the need for it with an integrated lesson experience.**
