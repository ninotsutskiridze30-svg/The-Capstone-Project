**HEADER:** TutorLink | The tutoring platform for Georgian exam-prep students | tutoring-lyart.vercel.app | June 2026

**PROBLEM (40 words max):**
Georgian high-school students spend days finding an exam-prep tutor through fragmented WhatsApp and Telegram group chats — with no visibility into tutor availability, rates, or exam-topic coverage. No Georgian-language tutoring platform exists.

**SOLUTION (30 words max):**
TutorLink is a two-sided marketplace where students find verified exam-prep tutors, book sessions, attend HD video lessons with a shared whiteboard, and submit homework — in Georgian or English.

**TRACTION (3 real numbers):**
