## ERRC Table

| Action | Factors | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Eliminate** | Global tutor supply as differentiator | We are not competing on breadth across 50 languages. TutorLink is deep on Georgian exam subjects, not broad globally. Maintaining a global supply catalogue at this stage would dilute focus and confuse our ICP. |
| **Reduce** | Platform commission vs Preply/Tutorful | 15% vs 18–33%. Deliberate positioning to win tutor trust, not a cost-cutting measure. Tutors are the supply side — losing them to off-platform arrangements is the biggest risk. |
| **Raise** | Exam-syllabus specificity | Exam coverage fields on tutor profiles — no competitor has this. Directly addresses the #1 student complaint from discovery: "I don't know if the tutor teaches the exam syllabus specifically." |
| **Raise** | Integrated lesson experience | Whiteboard + homework inside the same session, not bolted on. The whiteboard was identified unprompted as the key differentiator over Zoom in usability testing. |
| **Create** | Tutor referral loop | Tutors invite existing private students via unique link. No global platform has a supply-led acquisition channel built this way. Creates K-factor-driven growth with CAC of ₾1.32. |
| **Create** | AI-assisted homework grading tied to national exam | AI review of homework submissions against Georgian exam marking criteria. No competitor offers this. |

## Narrative (150 words)

TutorLink does not try to beat Preply or iTalki at their own game. Competing on global tutor supply, brand recognition, or paid acquisition would require resources and timelines a solo-founder MVP cannot support — and would produce a product that is generically worse than established players on their home turf.

The Blue Ocean move is to draw the competitive curve on completely different axes: Georgian-first language quality, exam-syllabus specificity, and an integrated lesson experience that makes every session demonstrably better than a plain Zoom call. These three factors are genuinely absent from every competitor and directly address the pains our ICP named in discovery interviews.

The ERRC logic is: eliminate the race for global supply breadth, reduce the commission to win tutor trust, raise the two dimensions that matter most to Georgian exam students, and create two new dimensions — the referral loop and AI exam grading — that did not exist in this market before.
